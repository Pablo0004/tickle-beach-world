# Checklist for the owner

Things done that only you can check on your own computer (the test browser has no real mouse, no controller and no
graphics card). Tick what looks right and note what does not. (This replaces the old Spanish `REVISAR.md`.)

## First person (version 50)

- [ ] With one of your Tickles in "I'm in control", press V: a click captures the mouse and you look round as in a
      shooter; Esc frees the mouse and a second Esc leaves.
- [ ] The mouse sensitivity: too fast or too slow?
- [ ] W/S forward and back, A/D sideways without turning (as in Counter-Strike), Shift to run, Space to jump.
- [ ] The head bob while walking: pleasant or dizzying?
- [ ] The mouse wheel takes the camera back behind the Tickle (an optional third-person view) and in again.
- [ ] The crosshair names what is in front of you; with the mouse captured, a click opens its window.
- [ ] The health and needs panel on the left, the compass at the top.
- [ ] The ability bar at the bottom (F, Q, G, R and T): try a warrior, a mage, a healer and one with nothing, near a
      cave with monsters. Are the effects clear? Are the cooldowns sensible?
- [ ] Monsters hit back; if you are knocked out, first person ends.
- [ ] T to talk to the Tickle in front.
- [ ] With a controller: the sticks, A to jump, X/Y/LB/RB the abilities, Start to leave. (If the browser does not
      let the artifact use the controller, say so.)
- [ ] On a phone: the stick on the left, drag on the right to look, the bar's buttons.
- [ ] Riding a horse or a griffin with M in first person (Space and C to climb and dive on the griffin).

## More detail (version 50, item 1.2)

- [ ] Menu > Settings > Graphics: try Normal, High and Ultra. Is it smooth on your computer in each? (High is the
      default.)
- [ ] The textures of the blocks (grass, sand, paths, planks, stone): is the grain right?
- [ ] Models near you with smaller cubes (trees, houses, fires) get finer as you come close; is the change of
      detail noticeable as you move (a "jump")?
- [ ] Sun shadows on High and Ultra; none at night.
- [ ] The sky's gradient by day, at dusk, at night and in a storm.
- [ ] The open sea's waves (outer beaches): made of cubes, taller, with foam.
- [ ] Castles, palaces and mansions close up: window frames and sills, door steps, courses of stone on the castles.
- [ ] Does the game load a little faster than before?

## Life in the villages

- [ ] At night, at a castle or a mansion: do they all go in? (Only the wall guards and those coming back from a
      trip should be outside.)
- [ ] The logs round the fire, on their side, facing it.
- [ ] Nobody walks through buildings: Tickles, animals, creatures and monsters go round houses, castles, stables,
      altars, the statue... and in and out by the door.
- [ ] The arenas with 4 gates and their towers; the walls with at least 4 gates.

## Under the sea (item 1.2c)

- [ ] With one of your Tickles in first person in the open sea: C to go down, Space to come up. Does the bottom look
      good (swaying kelp, coral in Beach Sand and The Palm, fish, turtles, mantas, jellyfish, crabs)?
- [ ] The breath bar: a minute and a half (and the "Tank" bar with an oxygen tank, see below).
- [ ] Follow a Tickle diving at a dive site: the view from above under the water, as in Pokemon Emerald.
- [ ] Find a sea cave (2 per region, out at sea): go down to its mouth and press E. From the surface it should not let
      you in.

## The menu (item 1.4)

- [ ] The yellow "Menu" button (top left) and Esc open it; Esc or "Back to the world" close it.
- [ ] With the menu closed the world fills the screen; do you miss anything on view?
- [ ] Every section: Tickles, Holders, Adventure, Map, Directory, Encyclopedia, Director, Review, Library, How it
      works, Controls, Sound, Settings, Credits.
- [ ] M opens and closes the map inside the menu; a click on the map takes you there and closes the menu.
- [ ] "Full card" in a Tickle's window opens the menu on its card.
- [ ] Would you rather the game paused while the menu is open? Today it keeps running.
- [ ] On a phone: the sections in a row at the top.

## More detail in the living (28 October)

- [ ] Go near a dragon asleep on its peak: wings folded on its back, lying down, breathing, a puff of smoke now and
      then. Woken, it opens its wings.
- [ ] Look at a dragon, a monster or a creature close up with Graphics on High and Ultra: finer cubes than on Normal.
- [ ] Go into a dungeon: the monsters sway as they walk, breathe standing still and lunge to strike.
- [ ] Check the game is still smooth on your computer on High.

## The encyclopedia (item 1.3)

- [ ] Menu > Encyclopedia: the beings you have not seen yet are dark with "???"; go near an animal, a creature or a
      monster and check the notice comes up and its entry fills in.
- [ ] Open an entry: its 3D model, number and data. Arms, spells, relics, mounts and the rest are open from the start;
      the War section has the units, the formations, the armoury, the catapult, the towers, the traps and the stars.
- [ ] Close the game and come back: what was seen is still seen.

## Wars (item 1.5)

- [ ] Go to a fortress: inside the walls there is an armoury and catapults. Inspect a building: its health shows.
- [ ] The castle's great gates: open by day, shut at night (they open when someone of the household arrives).
- [ ] Menu > World > Wars: the wars on, their armies, formations, the gate and the castle; click one to go there.
- [ ] Watch a war close up: the column marching along the road (cavalry in front, the ram at the head, catapults
      behind), the siege at the gate, the catapults on the walls throwing, the fallen going home.
- [ ] At the end: the convoy carries the spoils; the fallen get up when it arrives; the beaten place shows "held by"
      the winner.
- [ ] Stars: the war panel shows the stars and the % destroyed; a notice comes with each star. With 3 stars the winner
      keeps the place; with 2, everything but the place; with 1, half.
- [ ] Archers on the walls: in a siege the defenders with bows, the mages and the tamers go up beside the gate and
      shoot; the towers shoot arrows too.
- [ ] Once the gate is broken, the traps catch the first ones through (it shows in the log).

## Life indoors

- [ ] Go into a mansion or a castle: one ground floor with the great hall in the middle and the kitchen, the training
      rooms, the lounge and the treasury at the sides, behind low walls with doorways. The card says "On this floor:
      ...". The bedrooms are up the stairs.
- [ ] Outside meal times every Tickle in a place of its own (one per chair or sofa seat, reading at the shelf,
      looking out of the window, training, chatting in pairs, strolling), never in a row. The card says what each
      is doing. Those who chat stand a step apart, face to face.
- [ ] The casino at night: those not playing stand round the tables watching; in the tournament room they wait in
      little knots. In a guild hall, every member in a place of its own.
- [ ] The float and the wake board are not in the paws on land or indoors; in the water (swimming, floating) they
      are. The towel only near the water; snacks and chips always (indoors too).
- [ ] In the evening at a castle or a big mansion: some patrol the walls, some are at the armoury, many indoors; at
      the fire fewer, and further apart. No pets at the fire: they wait at the stable (bigger, further off) and come
      back with their Tickle afterwards.
- [ ] At night tamed creatures wait at the stable of their village or fortress, not at the door.
- [ ] More griffins: in castles and great estates some warriors fly a griffin; on the wall watch they patrol in the
      air above it. A griffin rider leaving or entering a fortress flies over the wall.
- [ ] When a war, raid, dragon attack, sea beast, tournament or Underworld expedition you are watching ends: a card at
      the top with the result (who won, the score or the stars, what they take). It closes by itself or with a click.
- [ ] Inside a place, the long description is folded into "About this place".

## Build mode and homes (1.6)

- [ ] In "I'm in control": Build with its modes (one, line, wall, fill, clear: two clicks), the ghost block (red where
      it is not allowed), every block type.
- [ ] Place object: choose a group and an object, see the ghost, R turns it. Move: pick an object up and set it down
      elsewhere. Remove: take it away. Undo or Ctrl+Z.
- [ ] Try on your own land (it stays), on another holder's (not allowed) and on neutral land (a notice; the
      Parliament votes on it at its afternoon sitting and, if it is thrown out, it goes).
- [ ] Choose your holder, go into one of your homes and press "Edit this home": move, turn and take away furniture;
      add a piece; change the floor and the walls; add a room; repaint the roof and go out to see it. Outside, in
      Place object, the "Garden and extras" group (flower beds, hedges, a porch).

## Oxygen tanks (1.6b) and the sea

- [ ] In first person under the water: the breath lasts a minute and a half; with a tank the "Tank" bar (10 minutes)
      is used first; it refills at the surface.
- [ ] A Tickle with a tank dives with the yellow tank on its back. Its card lists "Oxygen tank" among its things; the
      encyclopedia has it under Arms.
- [ ] Go near the coast or a dive site: the water lets you see the bottom; further off it goes back to its colour.
- [ ] Go into a sea cave (diving to its mouth): dark, dark blue stone, light only near the torches, crabs and
      jellyfish in the passages.

## Controls, first person inside, birds (30 October)

- [ ] Menu > Controls: everything in tables (keyboard, controller, touch). In first person the keys line no longer
      shows; nor does the dock's hint.
- [ ] First person inside: in "I'm in control", your Tickle in first person (V), go to a door and press E: you walk
      in, through its own eyes (the Tickle is not seen). Walk (WASD), look (mouse or arrows), it stops at walls and
      furniture; the door or stairs buttons show only when you reach them (E takes them). Go out of the door: you are
      still in first person outside. Esc: the room view.
- [ ] Birds in flight (arriving at their tree, or the gulls over the sea) spread and beat their wings.
- [ ] Trees and bushes in several greens.
